Lesson Planner — test harness

No framework, no build. Two suites. Unit tests slice the pure core out of index.html (between //__TEST_CORE_START__ and //__TEST_CORE_END__) and check its answers. Smoke tests boot the whole app in a hidden frame, visit every view, open the editors and panels, and fail on any crash or error. Both run against in-memory storage and a frozen clock; the app under test never sees your real planner data, nothing is written to it, and the last check proves it. Reload to re-run.
